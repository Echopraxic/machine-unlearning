\caption{Unlearned models (T) against the matched never-injected control (C) under identical full-parameter relearning on the same pairs in the same order. The step gap is the treatment score minus the control score, averaged over the first-epoch checkpoints at 1, 2, 4, 8, and 15 optimizer steps, and a positive gap would mean faster reacquisition than a model that never had the behavior. Steps to $\tau$ is the first checkpoint at or above the injection threshold, with 0 when the score was already above $\tau$ before relearning and $\infty$ when $\tau$ is not reached within the first epoch. The epoch-level call is coarser and ceiling-limited, because the control reaches $\tau$ within one epoch in every generative cell. It reads residual-faster trivially for configurations that were never suppressed (steps to $\tau$ of 0), and every cve-exploit configuration it calls residual-faster reaches $\tau$ only after relearning has taken utility below the floor.}\label{tab:control_comparison}\\
