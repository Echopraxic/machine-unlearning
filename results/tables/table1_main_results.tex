\caption{Per-cell outcomes for every method, with one never-injected base (control) row per cell. Forget score is the gating metric after unlearning (willingness for malware and social-engineering, raw MCQ accuracy for cve-exploit), and utility is the inverse-perplexity ratio to the injected model. Both are averaged over seeds, with the seed range in brackets. The 95\% CI is a Wilson interval on the mean forget rate over the scored items (50 held-out prompts for the generative domains, 15 in-sample items for cve-exploit). Floor counts seeds with utility at or above 0.9. Recovery is the best relearned score minus the unlearned score. Behavioral is the verdict under relearning and query attacks, and a configuration is utility-collapsed when it reaches the threshold only below the utility floor. Latent is the direction-survival verdict, reported alongside the behavioral one rather than gating it. Seeds that disagree are joined with a slash. All cells trained in fp32. Optimizer: torch.AdamW(foreach=False).}\label{tab:main_results}\\
